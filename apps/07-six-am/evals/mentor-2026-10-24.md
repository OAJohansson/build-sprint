### E2 sources and "heard" claims (rule checks per answer)

An answer passes when all of: (a) every sentence has a source in {ears, uploader, web, profile,
library, general}; (b) every `web` sentence has an http(s) URL; (c) no sentence or listen-next
`because` / genre reason makes a first-person perception claim — regex
`\b(I|I've|we)\s+(hear|heard|listen|listened)\b|listening to (it|this)|sounds like|that bassline`
(case-insensitive), each hit then read by hand: "sounds like" counts only as a perception claim,
not in "what X sounds like" teaching phrasing about a genre; (d) the source label fits the sentence
(hand check: an `ears` sentence must state something in the Ears facts; a `profile` sentence
something in the profile). Length rule recorded separately: 2–4 sentences outside teach (1–2 for
listen_next per its instruction).

### E3 listen next (per pick)

Invented = the artist or the track title can't be found on any real page (the linked page or a web
search). A pick whose link page names a different artist/track than claimed counts as invented.
Link resolves = HTTP 2xx/3xx on GET with a browser user-agent; 403/429/Cloudflare challenge =
"unverifiable" (excluded from the denominator, reported). 404/410/DNS failure = not resolving.

### E4 setup profile (per playlist)

Pass when the draft profile (i) names the playlist's dominant genre family as seen in its tracks'
uploader genres/tags (≥ half the tracks), (ii) says nothing contradicted by the tracklist (e.g. a
genre that none of the tracks carry stated as a main taste), (iii) hedges ("so far", "seems") rather
than stating broad rules from one playlist, and (iv) its 3–5 questions are about this taste.
