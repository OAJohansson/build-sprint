# Drum kit samples: sources and licences

Every file here is released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication)**,
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. The licence was checked on each sound's own
Freesound page on 25 Oct 2026. CC0 needs no attribution; the authors are credited anyway.

The files are Freesound's own "HQ preview" transcodes (MP3, 128 kbps) of the CC0 originals, served
by `cdn.freesound.org`; the licence of the original applies. Nothing was re-encoded or edited here.

The "909" kit comes from a Roland JD-Xi's TR-909 sounds and the "808" kit from a Roland TR-08 (the
Boutique TR-808 re-creation): **909-style and 808-style**, not samples of the original machines.
