# Drum kit samples: sources and licences

Every file here is released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication)**,
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. The licence was checked on each sound's own
Freesound page (25 Oct 2026 for the 909 and 808 kits, 27 Nov 2026 for the Phase 7 kit). CC0 needs no
attribution; the authors are credited anyway.

The 909 and 808 files are Freesound's own "HQ preview" transcodes (MP3, 128 kbps) of the CC0
originals, served by `cdn.freesound.org`; the licence of the original applies. Nothing was re-encoded
or edited in those two kits (the Phase 7 kit was; see its section).

The "909" kit comes from a Roland JD-Xi's TR-909 sounds and the "808" kit from a Roland TR-08 (the
Boutique TR-808 re-creation): **909-style and 808-style**, not samples of the original machines.

## Phase 7 starter kit (`/kits/p7/`), 120 one-shots from many uploaders

Added 27 Nov 2026 (Phase 7, L266, L267, L271). Every file is **CC0 1.0**, checked on the sound's own
Freesound page on 27 Nov 2026. Unlike the two kits above, these files **were edited** (CC0 allows it):
from Freesound's HQ preview (MP3, 128 kbps) of each original, leading silence trimmed, a 2 ms fade-in,
the tail cut where it falls 60 dB under the peak or at the row's cap (kick, claps, percussion 1.5 s;
closed hat 1 s; open hat 2.5 s; ride 4 s) with a fade-out, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, mixed to mono
and re-encoded as MP3 128 kbps. Some recordings also ship with Sonic Pi (its samples are CC0 Freesound
sounds, `etc/samples/README.md` in github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi); their Sonic Pi name is noted.
Names such as "909", "808" or "CR-78" describe the style or the uploader's machine, not samples owned by
Roland or anyone else; the person who recorded each hit released it as CC0.
