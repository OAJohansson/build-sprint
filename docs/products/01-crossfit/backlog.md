Ideas and parked feedback for this product. Numbers in brackets refer to the
[feedback log](#01-crossfit.feedback).

## Start here next time

**Progress section on Home** (#9, also solves #5). The user test scored "see I'm progressing" 2/5
and "Am I getting stronger?" failed. Put it above the PBs, on one screen:
1. **Consistency:** training days per week for the last 8 weeks, plus "6 of 8 weeks at 3+".
2. **Lifts moving:** for each lift done 3+ times, a small chart of the top weight per session and
   one line like "Back squat +17.5 kg since 18 Aug".
3. **Benchmarks repeated:** "Fran 5:32 → 4:58 (−34 s)".

Tapping a lift opens its page with the same chart above the history. About 2–3 hours.
Three design options (P1–P3) are on the [design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/244xnmDMMpHvigepL751qX).
