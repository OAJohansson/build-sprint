# Persona: Sam, a CrossFitter who wants to stop guessing their numbers

## Who

- 32, office job, CrossFit 3–5 times a week for 4 years at one box. Intermediate: solid on the
  barbell, still chasing a bar muscle-up. Uses kg.
- Tech comfort high, patience low. Has tried spreadsheets, notes apps and a big fitness app; quit
  all of them within weeks because logging took longer than the workout cool-down.

## Moments of use

1. **Mid-class, between sets.** Coach says "build to 75% of your 1RM back squat". Chalky hands,
   phone on the floor or in a pocket, about 30 seconds before the next set, loud music.
2. **Right after class.** Sitting on a box or in the car, sweaty, 2–3 minutes before heading off.
   Wants to dump what they did and go.
3. **Sunday evening on the sofa.** Curious, relaxed, 5 minutes: "Was it a good week? Am I getting
   stronger?"
4. **First time.** Just installed it; will give it about one minute to prove itself.

## Jobs to be done

1. "When the coach calls a percentage, I want the weight to load in seconds, so I don't guess or
   hold the class up."
2. "When class is done, I want to log what we did in under a minute, so I keep a record without
   it feeling like homework."
3. "When I look back, I want to see that I'm progressing (heavier lifts, faster WODs, showing up),
   so I stay motivated."
4. "When I hit a PB, I want it recorded and celebrated, so the effort feels noticed."

## Frustrations

- Typing on a phone with sweaty hands. Forms with many fields.
- Having to pick an exercise from a long list every time.
- Apps that store everything but show nothing back ("data graveyard").
- Getting the maths wrong: 70% of 87.5 isn't a weight you can load.

## What "worth it" means

- After a week: never had to guess a percentage; logging took less than a minute a day.
- After a month: can point at a lift and say "up 7.5 kg since September", and sees how often they
  trained. Would show it to a friend at the box.

## Language they use

PB (also says PR), 1RM, 3RM, "a heavy single", "5 by 3", "EMOM", "AMRAP", "for time", "Rx",
"scaled", "the WOD", "the box". Says "kilos". Wouldn't say "entry", "record", "session title".
