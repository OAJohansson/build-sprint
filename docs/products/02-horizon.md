## Learned

- Google already answers "what time is sunset?"; the edge is "do I have time?" plus a sky beautiful enough to show a friend.
- The emotional peak (the sunset moment, then the afterglow) only showed up in testing; it's now the heart of the app.
- Guardrails need numbers: text that looked fine measured 1.9:1 until colour was picked against the sky behind it.
