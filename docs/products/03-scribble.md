## Learned

- The riskiest assumption was emotional (perfectionism), so every design choice had to lower the bar to start: feedback on request, one lesson, a forgiving goal.
- A feel is evoked, not drawn: typewriter type, ink and rhythm worked; three rounds of illustrated typewriters didn't.
- The cheapest learning feature was the best one: yesterday's lesson as one quiet line above today's page.
