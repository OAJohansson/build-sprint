## Theme prompts

One line per theme where this product has a real example; leave the rest empty.

- **Discovery:** *Where did the real problem turn out different from the request?* 
- **Prioritisation:** *What did I decide not to build, and why?* 
- **Metrics:** *How did I know it worked? Which number moved?* 
- **Failure:** *What did I get wrong, and what did I change because of it?* 
- **Ambiguity:** *Which decision did I make with incomplete information, and how did I reduce the risk?* 
- **Trade-offs:** *What did I give up to get something else?* 
- **Craft:** *Which detail made it feel great to use?* 
- **Technical:** *What technical problem did I solve, and how would I explain it simply?* 
- **Working with AI:** *What did AI do well, and where did I have to correct or overrule it?* 
- **Speed:** *How did I ship fast without lowering the bar?*
