## Learned

- The real problem was the 15-minute call, not "a tracker": a one-screen summary since the last appointment, plus relief linked to each dose, is what tells patient and clinician whether the medicine works.
- Time is a test dimension: two of four major findings (relief not asked on resume, a moved appointment emptying the summary) only appeared when the tester let time pass.
- In health, words are claims: describe a feeling (Mallow), show the user's own data, never promise relief.
