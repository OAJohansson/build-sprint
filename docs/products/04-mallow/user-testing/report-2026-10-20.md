## Value

- **Earns its place:** the 1-tap oil tick and 2-tap vape log; the sleep graph with the medicine strip; the Summary sheet; the calm "Did your appointment happen?" prompt.
- **Doesn't (yet):** the Journey caption (states levels, hides change); Know your medicine (read once, then rarely); the plant (she doesn't notice it).
- **Missing that would bring her back tomorrow:** a line that says in words what changed ("sleep eased through August and has held"), and vape use per week so she can see it without feeling judged.

## Progress

She can see: a 3-month line per symptom, the before level, what she was on, and a Summary comparing her last stretch with the one before. She can't see: change put into words on Journey, how her vape use is trending, or anxiety before and after a plan change. Most valuable view to add: a **"Then → now" row under the Journey graph**, one line per symptom ("Before: poorly · first month: poorly · last 4 weeks: a bit broken"), plus "Vape: used 3–4 days a week since 6 Sep" under the strip. It answers "is it helping, and since when?" in the doctor's words, with no causal claim.

## Top recommendations

1. Rewrite the Journey caption as a then → now change with dates, aligned with the Summary (F1, F5).
2. Leave vape "Started" and the steppers blank, and offer "add another" in setup (F6, S1a, S1b).
3. Separate appointment ticks from date ticks and label the vape start on the strip (F7).
4. Expire last night's relief question, and show a "better" direction cue on the axis (F4, F3).
5. Label the plan "Last night" between midnight and 04:00 (F2).

## Method notes

Simulated: the follow-up call (the agent played Priya and Dr Okafor), time passing (`page.clock`: 20 Oct 09:00 → 25 Oct 08:30 → 26 Oct 00:30), and times of day (22:40, 08:15, 20:30). The demo is generated relative to the clock when loaded and does not regenerate on reload, but each fresh load shifts the dates. The Next.js dev-tools badge covers the bottom-left corner in screenshots. Times are estimates for Priya, not script timings. Medical accuracy of the cards not judged. Suggestion: a hand-count script in the plan that prints period averages from localStorage. Screenshots stayed in the session's scratch folder (not committed).
