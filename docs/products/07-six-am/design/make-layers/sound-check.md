# Sound check: are Make's layers, presets and knobs the ones producers use? (28 Oct)

The owner's canvas comment: "I don't know much about music theory… is that the main makeup of FX?
Make sure we've looked at other music production boards and that the different filters make sense."
Checked against the Roland TR-909 (the drum machine behind house), sample-pack FX categories
(Loopmasters, Wave Alchemy) and producer tutorials (Attack Magazine, Point Blank, FaderPro, Syntorial).

## Drums

| Row | Our knobs | Real reference | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kick | Tune, Decay, Click, Drive | TR-909 kick: Tune, Attack (really a click), Decay, Level | Right. Drive is the modern extra producers add. |
| Clap & snare | Tone, Decay, Spread, Room | 909 snare: Tune, Tone, Snappy (the snare-wire noise); 909 clap: level only | Change: **Spread → Snap** (how much noise/crack), the one control both claps and snares have. |
| Hats | Tone, Length, Swing, Width | 909 hats: a decay per hat (closed/open) | Change: **drop Swing**. The swing is copied from the original with the pattern (L215); a knob for it would fight the copy. Three knobs. |

Gap: rides, crashes, shakers, congas and toms have no row; they land in the hats or kick row.
That is fine for a first copy; a fourth "Percussion" row (the Studio already has the lane) goes to the backlog.

## Melody / chords

| Our presets | Our knobs | Real reference | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stab, Pluck, Pad, Keys | Tone (the filter), Length, Attack, Detune, Width, Space | House stabs, plucks, pads, organ/piano: a low-pass filter plus a short envelope do most of the work; pads = longer sustain, chorus, reverb | Right. Tone is the filter cutoff, Length/Attack the envelope, Space the reverb. |

## FX

FX are the sounds that mark the joins between parts of a song, not notes or beats: they build
tension before a drop, land it, and let the energy down after.

| Type | What it does | In our design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Riser (uplifter) | rises in pitch/noise into a drop | Noise riser ✓ |
| Downlifter (fall) | falls after a peak to release energy | Downlifter ✓ |
| Impact (hit) | marks the first beat of the drop | Impact ✓ |
| Reverse cymbal | a swell into the beat, the organic riser | add as a **Riser preset** ("Reverse crash") |
| Sub drop | a low boom falling under the impact | add as an **Impact preset** ("Sub drop") |
| Sweep | white noise filtered up or down | already what Noise riser / Downlifter do |
| Atmosphere / texture (vinyl, ambience) | a bed under a section | not FX; the Studio's Texture lane, backlog |

So the three rows are the main makeup of house FX; two common sounds join as presets, not rows.
