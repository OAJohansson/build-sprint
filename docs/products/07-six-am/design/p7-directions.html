Six AM · Rebuild the song, ten directions

Phase 7 Discover (decision 0028). Each direction shows the same three moments on the same demo track (Nora Vale, Hold On (Club Mix), 124 BPM, A minor, 96 bars: Intro 1–16, Build 17–32, Drop 1 33–64, Breakdown 65–80, Drop 2 81–96): 1 Make → Drums, choosing one of the kick's 20 sounds; 2 Make → Drums, fixing by ear (tap a square to add or take out a hit, M / S per row, click the bar numbers to play from there; Shaker / percussion and Ride start empty); 3 Studio → Rebuild the song (empty slots per section and lane, the original's parts as a hint for kick, hats, bass, chords and vocals only, filling a slot from saved parts, Mine / Original), plus the Studio when the Deck isn't open. Rough prototypes: nothing plays, but taps work. All copy is draft. Open p7-directions/prototype.html in a browser: 1–0 or ←/→ switch direction, the buttons top right switch the moment; add &data=worst for long names. Pick three.

1

Fold

Today's Make room kept: six rows on top, the knob block under them, the Mentor rail. The 20 sounds open as a popover from the sound's name, in family columns. Empty rows are striped with the 'Your turn' line written across them. Studio: today's lanes, each section header says how many parts are still to fill and what the original has.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=1 (moments 1–3 top right)

2

Shelf

No Mentor rail. The 20 sounds sit on an open shelf under the rows, one column per family. Rows Make heard sit on top; the empty rows wait in their own group below a dashed line, each with its 'Your turn' line and 'Leave empty'. Studio: a board, one card per section (wider for longer sections), every lane listed in it.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=2 (moments 1–3 top right)

3

Sound map

The 20 sounds placed on a map you can read by ear: dark to bright, short to long; tap a dot to hear it. Empty rows get a gold edge and a question ('Shaker?'). Studio: the original's parts show as faint marks inside each lane, bar for bar, under your slots.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=3 (moments 1–3 top right)

4

Side list

The Mentor rail becomes the sound list: families as headings, each sound with its feel word and one plain line. Empty rows are named in one gold banner above the grid with 'Go to' buttons. Studio: saved parts on the left; above the lanes, a matrix of what the original has per section (filled dot = yours, ring = to fill).

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=4 (moments 1–3 top right)

5

Focus

One row big at a time. A list of the six rows on the left says each row's state in words (Make heard it / Yours to fill by ear). Sounds: family tabs, then that family's sounds. Studio: one section at a time, picked from a strip of sections; every lane says what the original has there.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=5 (moments 1–3 top right)

6

Checklist

The drums as six jobs with a progress line ('4 of 6 rows'); empty rows are unticked to-dos. Sounds in two steps: pick a kind (with its feel words), then a sound. Studio: the song as a to-do list down the page, one card per section with chips for what's filled and what's to fill.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=6 (moments 1–3 top right)

7

Machine

A drum machine: big pads, one bar at a time, with the whole loop as a thin strip above to click anywhere. Sounds on a wheel of 20 (family colours) with the current one in the middle and next and back buttons. Empty rows are striped pads; a '2 rows open' readout. Studio: time runs down the page like a tracker, lanes as columns.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=7 (moments 1–3 top right)

8

Twin

Every row shows what Make heard as a thin line above what you play, so a removed or added hit shows against the reading. Sounds in one strip from dark to bright. Studio: the original's measured parts stacked above your lanes, bar for bar, with a big Mine / Original switch between them.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=8 (moments 1–3 top right)

9

Mentor

The Mentor rail leads: a pinned note names the empty rows and why the loop sounds thinner, with 'Go to' buttons. Sounds picked by feel word first (punchy, warm, dusty...). Studio: the Mentor rail says which section to fill next and lists what's left per section.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=9 (moments 1–3 top right)

10

Table

Plain rows and columns: a State column for each row ('17 hits, heard' / 'empty, yours to fill'). The 20 sounds as a table with kind, feel, short-long and dark-bright bars, and a one-line description. Studio: a song strip with small bars per measured part (outline = in the original, filled = yours) and an inspector for the picked section.

Open: p7-directions/prototype.html?v=10 (moments 1–3 top right)
