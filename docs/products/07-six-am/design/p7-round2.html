Six AM · Rebuild the song, round 2: the owner’s three

Phase 7 design round 2 (decision 0028, L276): 2 Shelf, 4 Side list and 10 Table, developed on the same demo track (Nora Vale, Hold On (Club Mix), 124 BPM, A minor, 96 bars: Intro 1–16, Build 17–32, Drop 1 33–64, Breakdown 65–80, Drop 2 81–96). Every drum sound is the real kit (kit-p7.ts, 120 CC0 sounds, names from their ids, dials from their measured length and brightness). Six states each at 1440 × 900 (click a picture) with the 1280 × 720 version linked under it. Rough prototype: nothing plays, but taps work. All copy is draft for the owner to rewrite. 2

Shelf

The 20 real sounds sit on a shelf under the rows, one column per family (the 909 ride family says “stand-ins”), the picked sound’s plain line and knobs above it, “Sound credits” beside the count. Rows Make heard sit on top; Shaker / percussion and Ride wait below a gold dashed line with “Your turn: listen for a shaker and tap it in · Leave empty” under each, and the reason in one line (Make can’t hear them; some hits may sit on Closed hat). Fixing by ear folds the shelf to one line so the rows get the height. Studio: one card per section, wide as its bars, “N to fill” on top, gold “to fill” on the measured lanes, a key line above; the picked slot’s parts and “Leave empty” on the right.

Side list

The right rail is the 20 real sounds with their feel word and their one plain line, families as headings (909 ride “stand-ins”), credits at its foot. One gold banner names what’s still open and why; each empty row also says it on its own strip, with “Leave empty”. Row names carry their sound or “to fill by ear”. Studio: one header per section shows what the original has (○ to fill, ● yours, · vocals not yet), lanes below; saved parts and the picked slot on the left.

Table

A State column says each row’s state in words (“17 hits · Make heard them”, “Empty: yours to fill · Leave empty”). The 20 real sounds as a table with kind, feel, short ↔ long and dark ↔ bright drawn from the measured length and brightness, sortable by those columns, and the full plain line in the side panel with the knobs and credits. Studio: a song strip (small bars per measured part) and an inspector table for the picked section: the original, yours, Fill… / Leave empty.
