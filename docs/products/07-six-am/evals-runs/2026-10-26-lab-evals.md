# Lab evals (Phase 4), 26 Oct 2026 (data-evals)

Graded against `evals.md` "Evals: Six AM Lab (Phase 4)". I graded only and changed no product code.
Setup: the sandbox dev server on :3047 (in-memory store, access code `test-code`). A launched Chrome
155 with its own profile and CDP on :9493, auto tab capture of "Six AM Deck", and `?quiet=1`. Track:
Mason Flint remix of *It Just Won't Do* (126.05 BPM, G minor, 108 bars, 3:26).

the session scratchpad, under `ev/`.

## Findings

- **F1 (known weakness, confirmed): Suggest settings goes to extremes.** 95 of 120 suggested macros
  are at 0–0.05 or 0.95–1, and pitchDrop ≥ 0.95 in 13 of 20 suggestions. Four of five loops read a
  9–15 semitone "pitch drop" in the original bass: kick bleed in the separated bass, as L173
  predicted. Wobble also maxes out (0.95–1) on Round sub and Rubber pluck. The distance does fall,
  but the gold ticks point at settings that likely sound unlike the bass. Likely fix: gate the drop
  feature on note pitch confidence or high-pass the kick band before measuring; clamp the search.
- **F2: the Reese A/B is level-matched in stereo and 1.1 dB quieter in mono** (Width layer). It is
  within the bar on `rmsDb`, but `mineLevel()` and any mono meter disagree. Either is fine; say
  which one the A/B promises.
- **F3 (UI, library):** "Your sounds from this track" shows the 4 newest plus "+6". Older saved
  sounds can't be loaded from Make (Eval Y5 one was saved and is in the API, but isn't visible).
  The sandbox library also holds a garbled name from an earlier test, "Rubbery bass, from TRubber
  sub, UT testim Deluxe", which looks like typing into the name field while it was being
  re-suggested. Worth a user-tester look.
- **F4:** the map's bar grid is ~35–55 ms after the real downbeat (Y8 observation). Loops cut there
  carry the first 55 ms of the next bar's kick, consistently, so the loop stays seamless.
