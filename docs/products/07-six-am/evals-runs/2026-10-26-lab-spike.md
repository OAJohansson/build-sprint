# Lab spike (L144), 26 Oct 2026

Machine: the owner's MacBook (Apple M5 Pro), Chrome 155.0.8059.85, a launched Chrome with its own
profile driven over CDP (`--auto-select-tab-capture-source-by-title="Six AM Deck"`, so no share
dialog). Spike page `/dev/lab-spike` (dev only), branch `claude/07-six-am-lab-4c61e0`.

Stack (checked from official sources): ONNX Runtime Web 1.30.0 (WebGPU EP, in a worker);
`demucs-web` 1.0.2 (MIT) pre/post-processing; model `timcsy/demucs-web-onnx`
`htdemucs_embedded.onnx` (Meta's htdemucs weights, MIT, 180.5 MB, 7.8 s window at 44.1 kHz stereo),
cached in Cache Storage after the first download (30 s first load, 0.6 s from cache).
`suppressLocalAudioPlayback`: Screen Capture constraint, Chrome 109+ (MDN).

Design: the SoundCloud widget plays in its own small "Six AM Deck" window, because
`suppressLocalAudioPlayback` silences the whole captured tab; the Lab tab captures the Deck and
plays only the separated parts. Rolling plan (`src/lib/lab/stream.ts`, 5 unit tests): each step
separates the latest 7.8 s window and plays its newest `hop` seconds, kept `guard` = 0.5 s back from
the window's edge, 30 ms crossfades, at a fixed delay.

playing, GPU shared) but still ~3× real time. Shorter hops cut the delay at the cost of headroom.

## (a) Original silent locally: pass

**Owner by ear (L151): pass, only Six AM's version heard.** `suppressLocalAudioPlayback` reads **true** on the captured track; the capture is not silent
(input −9 dB RMS). Whether the original is truly inaudible on the speakers needs the owner's ear.

## (c) Parts usable: pass (owner: drums, bass and other solos worked)

Bench, against the known parts of Six AM's own loop (SDR, higher is better; "mix as" = doing nothing):
drums **34.6 dB** (mix as drums 8.2), bass **18.5 dB** (mix as bass −9.3), other 13.2 dB.
Live levels per part on the real track: drums −11, bass −16, other −30, vocals −20 dB RMS (the
track has a sung vocal). Synthetic parts separate more cleanly than real music; usefulness on a
loved track is the owner's call by ear.

## Rights (L143)

Windows and regions live in memory for one step (copied from a 12 s ring, transferred to the
worker, played once by an AudioBufferSourceNode, dropped). No MediaRecorder, no storage, no upload;
the only network fetch of the separator is the model. A grader re-checks this at the end (Y3).

## Engine hardening (after L152), same machine, same track, real capture

The spike's loop moved into `src/lib/lab/live.ts` (`LiveParts`), with `separator-client.ts` and
`sync.ts` (12 unit tests in `src/lib/lab`, with `stream.ts`). Changes: the heard playhead (the Deck's
position one delay ago, from its reports stamped on the audio clock); silent windows (a paused
Deck, < −70 dB) skip the model; a late region plays its remainder instead of being dropped; the
ring is zeroed on stop and each played buffer disconnects when done; a hidden tab gets a clear
error instead of Chrome's InvalidStateError.
