## Value
- **Earns its place:** FX placed at the right bars on a loop I love, with shapes that respond to the knobs, and saving a riser straight onto the FX lane. The Mentor naming my clap's or chords' ticks when I ask "why different?" is genuinely helpful.
- **Doesn't (yet):** FX with no ticks and no score feel like guesswork; the FX Mentor answer about the bass.
- **Missing that would bring them back tomorrow:** a one-line "what this FX does" and a "start here" preset when the original has none.

## Progress
In FX I can see where each effect sits and its length, and "✓ saved", but nothing about how close it is or whether it fits; the page says "no closeness score for FX". The most valuable addition: when a row has no ticks, a **"fits the drop" check** next to A/B that says in words whether my riser ends on the drop and is as loud as the drop's build (e.g. "ends on bar 41 ✓, a bit loud"). It answers "will this sound right when the drop hits?".

## Top recommendations
1. Send the open Make family as the Mentor's part, so FX answers start with the row (F1).
2. One plain line per FX row on what it does (F2).
3. Stable riser reading, "into the drop" wording, and a suggested preset when there are no ticks (F3).
4. Tags and drawn shapes that match the knobs (F4, F5).
5. Mentor answers without the repeated disclaimer, quoting on-screen words (F7, F8).
