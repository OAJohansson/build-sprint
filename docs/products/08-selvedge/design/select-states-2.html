Picking saves, in yellow

Three soft ways to mark a picked save with the logo's yellow. Click photos in each row to try it; three are picked to start.

DYellow dot

A small watercolour dot at the corner, as if you'd touched a highlighter tip to the page, and the faintest pencil frame. The quietest of the three.

EUnderlined

A soft highlighter stroke under the photo, the way you'd underline something you like in a magazine. It draws in as you pick.

FSelvedge thread

A selvedge is the finished edge of denim, marked with a coloured thread. A picked photo gets a fine yellow stitch down its edge, ending in a tiny knot: picking a save is stitching it into the board.
