Picking saves for a new board

Three ways to show a picked save. Click photos in each row to try it; nothing is picked to start.

APencil tick

A hand-drawn tick and a pencil frame. Every photo stays full colour; the tick says "in".

BTaped in

Unpicked photos sit flat and faded; a picked one comes alive, lifts and gets a strip of tape, like taping it into the sketchbook.

CHighlighter

A yellow highlighter wash behind a picked photo, and a handwritten number showing the order you picked them in.
