# User test: Selvedge, 2026-10-23

**Persona:** the owner, thirties, dresses well, saves all day on Android and makes boards on a MacBook · **Method:** simulated (agent), phone 390×844 for S1, laptop 1440×900 for S2–S5 · **Build:** local, in-memory store, branch `08-wrap-up` at aac3574

## Verdict

"Saving is effortless and the board tools are better than Canva: the cut-out on the sunglasses honestly made me smile. But I undid two accidental deletes, and when I came back later in the week both photos were gone from the board and from the download. I'd keep using it, but I'd stop trusting Undo until that's fixed."

## Jobs

| Job (from persona) | Score 1–5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Save a piece in two taps | 4 | A simulated share landed on top of the Inbox in 0.5 s. An offline share showed "Waiting for a connection" and arrived about 5 s after reconnecting, with nothing to tap. |

## Findings

| ID | Severity | What happened | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | major (data loss) | Delete then Undo (button and ⌘Z): the screen showed the piece, the stored board kept the delete; after reload the pieces were gone. An undo of a move wasn't saved either. | Save after undo and redo like any other edit. |
| F2 | major | The pill could be dragged past the board (x 1041 + w 640 on 1600): text clipped, resize handle off-screen. | Keep pieces inside the board on move and resize. |
| F3 | minor | After a cut-out, the empty area around the subject still selects the piece. | Make only the visible subject clickable. |
| F4 | minor | The hide handle shows only on hover; the › to bring the sidebar back is 45% opacity (2.59:1). ⌘\ only in a tooltip. | Raise the › to about 70% and widen its hover zone. |
| F5 | minor | The pick bar covers the bottom row of cards; nothing says how many are picked. | Pad the grid bottom; show a count. |
| F6 | minor | On the laptop, Move to… opens as a full-width sheet at the bottom left. | On wide screens, a small menu by the button. |
| F7 | minor | Quiet grey text (#8f7e6c on #f7f0e3) is 3.45:1: "TODAY", "↶ Undo", "Saving…", "+ New board", captions. | Darken the quiet tone to at least 4.5:1, at least for Undo and Saving…. |
| F8 | polish | "Getting the scissors ready…" counted to 100%, dropped to 1%, then climbed again (two files). | One combined progress figure. |
| F9 | polish | The empty board gives no hint; "Inspo" link, "Inbox" tab and "Inspo" heading name one place three ways. | A one-line empty-board hint. |
| F10 | polish | Crop handles are 8–22 px. | Hit areas of about 24 px. |
| F11 | polish | The focus ring stays after a mouse click and looks like an "on" state. | Ring only for keyboard focus. |
| F12 | polish | The waiting card shows "Photo" with no picture. | A small thumbnail. |

**Guardrail (no save lost):** 2 of 2 shares arrived (online 0.5 s; offline queued, then arrived ~5 s after reconnecting); 2 of 2 laptop uploads arrived; after sorting, 22 saves with 22 unique ids (18 Inbox, 4 folder). On the board, 2 of 2 Undo restores were lost (F1).

## Value

- **Earns its place:** share-sheet capture with the offline queue, Remove background, Crop that keeps the photo's shape, shape colours from the board's photos, the pill title, the sharp PNG download.
- **Doesn't (yet):** Rough edge and Forward / Back (rarely used); colour notes in folders are pretty but say little.
- **Missing:** a shelf of boards, so two seasons can be seen side by side (the brief's "change over time").

## Top recommendations

1. Save after every undo and redo (F1).
2. Keep pieces inside the board (F2).
3. Make only the visible part of a cut-out clickable (F3).
4. Picking in the Inbox: room under the grid, a count, Move to… by its button on desktop (F5, F6).
5. Darken the quiet grey to 4.5:1 and make the › easier to see (F7, F4).

## Method notes

Share sheet simulated by a multipart POST to `/share-target`; offline by cutting the network for the service worker only; Chrome's own eyedropper can't open headless, so the fallback picker was tested. Couldn't judge: the real tap count on Android, a trackpad's feel, switching boards. Suggestion: an automatic reload-and-compare (stored board vs screen) after every editor scenario; F1 wouldn't have survived it.
