# Finch walkthrough (28 Nov)

The owner installed Finch and captured 56 screens (`finch-screens/`, numbered in order): onboarding
(01–27), first day and streak (28–31), home, adventure and growth (32–36, 56), profile, quests and
menu (36–38), goals, suggestions and insights (39–55). Animation and haptics aren't visible in stills.

## Owner's take

- Cute, nice UI; small **module cards** throughout; animation and **haptics** make it feel alive.
- Feels more like a game for kids than a serious, research-backed way to change behaviour: habits
  generate energy, energy buys clothes and adventures; there is a shop. "Feels like a scam."
- Likes the **getting-to-know-you onboarding** (tailored suggestions) and the **areas** (calm,
  cleaning, connection, gratitude, hygiene, movement, nutrition, productivity, self-kindness, sleep).
- But **bloated**: "so much going on". Take inspiration, keep it focused.

**Where it lands for Wonty:** Finch proves the format (a character, small cards, motion and haptics,
tiny first wins). Its weak spots are the ones Wonty is built on: no goals beyond today, streaks of
app opens, a currency loop and a lot of surface. Wonty: same warmth, adult and calm, research-backed,
one loop.
