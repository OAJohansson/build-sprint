# Persona: Andreas, five days into getting up at 7 and two days off nicotine pouches

Use they/them. Built from the kickoff pack's "Who" and moments of use; the persona is the owner.
Details the pack doesn't give are marked *(assumed)*.

## Who

- An adult in a career transition: building 20 products in 20 days while job hunting, so most days
  have no fixed morning commitment. *(assumed from the sprint context, not the pack)*
- Day 5 of waking at 7am. Day 2 without nicotine pouches (quit 23 Oct). Also wants to read and
  write in the evening.
- Knows what they should do and has read the research (habits take about two months; coming back
  counts more than streaks). What they lack is something that keeps them honest with themselves and
  keeps one bad day from ending the attempt.
- Tech comfort high, patience for setup moderate, patience for daily logging very low: it has to be
  one tap. Has tried quit and streak apps (I Am Sober, Streaks) and knows the reset-to-zero feeling.

## Moments of use

1. **Morning, on waking** (in bed or just up, phone in one hand, half awake): did I get up on time?
   A few seconds of attention at most.
2. **Evening, before bed** (often late, sometimes after midnight, tired): did I read, write, stay
   off pouches? Wants to close the day honestly in under a minute.
3. **A craving hits** (any time, restless, maybe out of the house): wants something to do with the
   moment other than reaching for a pouch, and to note it happened.
4. **The morning after a slip** (slept in, or had a pouch yesterday; feeling flat, tempted to skip
   the app): the moment the product is built for.

## Jobs to be done

1. "When I slip (a late morning, a pouch), I want to log it honestly and be met with encouragement
   and one next step, so I can keep one slip from becoming a lost week."
2. "When I start a habit, I want to write it as a clear commitment to myself (after I ___, I will
   ___, with a daily goal I can't fudge), so I know each day whether I kept it."
3. "When I've kept my commitment, I want to log it in one tap and feel a small, satisfying moment,
   so I can see my progress build."

## Frustrations

- A streak that resets to zero: one slip and the number says you failed.
- Guilt copy, red crosses, "you broke your streak", trophies and coins.
- Nagging alarms; apps that want to be the reason you do the habit.
- A checkbox list: rows with tick boxes feel like a chore list, not a promise.
- "21 days to a habit" and "1% better" slogans; they know these are myths.
- Anything per tap that animates or slows the log down; forms when they just want to tap.
- Losing history or having a typo stuck in a commitment they see every day.

## What "worth it" means

- **After a week:** they logged every day, including the bad one, and the bad day didn't feel like
  the end; the numbers dented instead of resetting.
- **After a month:** still waking at 7 most days (5 of 7) and haven't gone back to pouches. The
  history shows slips with comebacks after them.
- They'd recommend it if it got them through the first slip without the "I've blown it" spiral.

## Language they use

- Uses: "commitment", "promise to myself", "kept", "missed", "slip", "clean day", "craving",
  "urge", "come back", "after I ___, I will ___", "up by 7", "pages".
- Confusing or off-putting: "streak", "broken", "failed", "relapse", "XP", "level up",
  "challenge", "goal crushed", "implementation intention", "SRBAI", "automaticity".
