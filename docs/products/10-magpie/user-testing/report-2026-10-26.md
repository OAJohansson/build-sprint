## Value
- **Earns its place:** search by restaurant or note (weekly, in shops), capture with the saved moment (every good meal), the sticker/photo toggle.
- **Doesn't (yet):** the filter sheet is rarely needed because search and the shelves do most of it; Maggie's monthly line is cute but not actionable.
- **Missing that would bring them back tomorrow:** a shelf per trip or city ("Taipei · Oct · 2 yums").

## Progress
He sees a yum count, "yum no." and a monthly line, but not where he has eaten or which trips he logged. Most valuable addition: a **Trips row** under Latest, one card per away city and month, opening that trip's yums in date order.

## Top recommendations
1. Focus Name after the shutter; show sub-kinds without picking a kind first (F5, F1).
2. Prefill the city on shared photos (F4).
3. A Trips row on the shelf (Relive).
4. Placeholders while images load (F3).
5. Search kind words; fix the no-filter empty copy (F2).
