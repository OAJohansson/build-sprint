## Learned

- AI-written content needs a pipeline, not a prompt: a saved source corpus, citations on every item and a separate grader took it from 282 unsupported claims and 3 wrong ones to 0.
- The owner's canvas comments, made before any code, removed four things (dates on the globe, the "Whole field" pill, the themes popover, the welcome line) that would otherwise have been rebuilt.
- The map answers "where does this fit"; whether it makes me hop for weeks is still my own test, and "next on your thread" is the missing nudge.
