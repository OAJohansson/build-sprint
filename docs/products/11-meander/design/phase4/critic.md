# Design critic, phase 4 (music slice)

## Round 1 (27 Nov, sample content): 6/10
Scorecard: clutter 1/2 · hierarchy 1/2 · legibility 1/2 · feel 2/2 · copy 1/2

Reads as: "a quiet star-map reference for music history with a short list of records to look up; clearly the same product as philosophy, but the new header and the Listen rows are not settled yet."

Gaps, most important first:
1. The field switch and the view tabs look the same (four tabs, two selected). → Field in the serif next to the wordmark; underline only on Globe/Timeline.
2. "Listen" promises sound but opens a search without saying where. → Name the destination in the meta line.
3. "Listen" sits right above "Listen for". → Rename the records.
4. Listen pushes Step inside down; three Step inside styles. → One order and one style on both fields.
5. Three formats for a recording row; the artist view's is weakest. → Reuse the card's two-line row.
6. Globe and timeline look empty with 4 sample genres. → Wait for the real content.

Keep: the record row itself (no art, no play buttons); the skeleton's facts row.
