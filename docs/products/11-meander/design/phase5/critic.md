# Phase 5 globe: design critic

## Round 1 (28 Nov): A1 5/10, A3 5/10

A1 does the first-open job better ("see where everything sits" needs the back half nameable and reachable); A3 is
closer to the feel but taught wrong geography (family names at whole-family centroids, back names illegible).
Shared: thread hairball, threads cut around the title, philosophy opened on its 4 Eastern schools.

Fixed after round 1:
- A1: a family stays lit while open (members bright, the rest at back-dot faintness, non-members' names hidden);
  the list is quieter (no count, smaller and dimmer, bottom-left, member list scrolls); philosophy's list is its
  five eras instead of Western/Eastern.
- A3: a family name sits at the middle of its *front* members, only with two or more there, tries 7 nearby spots and
  gives way to any label or dot; back-side names removed; names are larger, full brass, and clickable (turns to the
  family and lights it); genre names a step dimmer, so families read first.
- Both: threads at rest at a third of their old strength; first open faces the densest side; the A globes are 8%
  smaller and 22px lower; the glow one step flatter; unlabelled front dots dimmer; threads now run up to the title
  text instead of stopping at an oval round it.

Not changed (the card and title belong to the signed-off design): the card's clipped bottom row, the title under the
focus dot, the dotted "provoked it" line, the "Globe"/"Genres" tab names.

## Round 2 (28 Nov): A1 8/10, A3 9/10

A1: hierarchy, feel and copy each up a point; the family-open state works ("the best frame in the set" for
philosophy's Contemporary). Left: in-family threads a new hairball, the list jumped and hid members, the eyebrow.
A3: clutter, hierarchy, legibility and feel up; left: hidden families have no name or route (3 of 8 named on first
open), the open family's name reads as a label's heading, no family names on the phone.
Critic's verdict: A1 does the first-open job narrowly better, A3 is the better-looking screen; strongest is both
together. So I built **A4 = A1's list + A3's front family names**, with the r2 fixes: lit-family threads at ~40%,
the list anchored from its top (family names never move), a fade at the end of a long member list, no eyebrow, the
list using the globe's short names, and other families' names hidden while one family is open.
Rounds stop here (the score reached the 9 target); the final pick is the owner's.
