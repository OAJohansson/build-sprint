“No system can live your life for you.”
Existentialism · 1840s–1960s · here now, 12 Nov

What it keeps asking

  1. If nothing hands me a purpose, how do I live?
  2. What does it mean to be free, and to answer for it?
  3. How do I live as myself and not as the crowd expects?

Keeps coming back to

  • Freedom and responsibility
  • Anxiety
  • Authenticity and bad faith
  • Existence before essence
  • The individual against the crowd

Who said it

  • Søren Kierkegaard1813–1855Truth has to be lived by one person. Faith is a leap.
  • Friedrich Nietzsche1844–1900God is dead, so values are ours to make. A forerunner.
  • Jean-Paul Sartre1905–1980Existence precedes essence. We are condemned to be free.
  • Simone de Beauvoir1908–1986Freedom always has a situation. One becomes a woman.
  • Albert Camus1913–1960The absurd, and revolt against it. He refused the label.

It was saying no to

German Idealism Hegel's system, which explains everything except the person living inside it, and the rationalism that puts abstract reason before a lived life.
