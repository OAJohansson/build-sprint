A · Clew

A clew is a ball of thread, the one you unwind to find your way. The whole field is one clew, turned so the school you are in faces you.

B · Red thread

The red thread: the line that runs through everything you have read and holds it together. The field is set like a page; your own path is the only colour on it.

C · Mobile

A mobile: every school hangs from the ones it grew out of, and the whole thing turns to bring the one you are in to the centre. Paper, thread and soft daylight.
