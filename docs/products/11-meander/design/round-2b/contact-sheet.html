A · Clew

A clew is a ball of thread, the one you unwind to find your way. The whole field is one clew, turned so the school you are in faces you.

B · Red thread

The red thread: the line through everything you have read. It leaves a knot at each school you visited, and its loose ends reach for the ones you have not.

C · Mobile

A mobile. The school you are in hangs at the top; what is one step away hangs from its arm, and the rest of the field hangs below that. Open another school and the whole thing re-hangs from there.
