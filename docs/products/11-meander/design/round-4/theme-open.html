Existentialism

1840s–1960s · you are here

It asks

  1. If nothing hands me a purpose, how do I live?
  2. What does it mean to be free, and to answer for it?
  3. How do I live as myself, and not as the crowd expects?

Themes

  • Lying to yourself that you have no choice: hiding behind a role, a rule or “that is just how I am”. The opposite is owning what you choose.
