**The critic's pick:** A3 with B1, plus these changes:
- Put the school name at the clew.
- Put the big question alone on the right, with no box.
- Keep the thinker lines under each idea, and drop "You'll meet" and the stat line.
- Add "idea 3 of 6" to the picked state.
- Fix the tight, thin display-serif tracking first.

**Main gaps in A1:** the neighbour labels mean you haven't really stepped inside the school, the radial layout gives no reading order, and the globe arcs cross the labels.

**Sketch errors that go away in the build:** the neighbour words and count differ from the live app. The app shows Phenomenology "inspired it", German Idealism "provoked it", and Feminist Philosophy and Post-structuralism "grew from it". The build reads the real data.
