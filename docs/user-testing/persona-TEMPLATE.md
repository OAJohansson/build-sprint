# Persona: [Name], [one-line who]

Use they/them. Specific beats broad: one real-feeling person, not a market segment.

## Who

- Age, life stage, how long they've done [the activity], skill level.
- Tech comfort and patience: [high/low], what apps they already use for this.

## Moments of use

When, where and in what state they open the app (time available, hands, attention, environment).

1. [Moment 1]
2. [Moment 2]

## Jobs to be done

What they're trying to get done, in their words. Ranked.

1. "When [situation], I want [outcome], so I can [motivation]."

## Frustrations

What makes them abandon an app like this (forms, typing, jargon, slowness…).

## What "worth it" means

The value they'd notice after a week and after a month. What would make them recommend it.

## Language they use

Words they'd use vs. words that would confuse them.
